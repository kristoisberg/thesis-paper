# ESE citation locator check

Date: 2026-08-28

Status: no manuscript change required.

ESE uses Springer author-year citations and does not prohibit pinpoint locators. Published ESE articles use page-qualified citations, and the repository's Springer Basic style documents optional page and chapter qualifiers.

The active paper contains 37 page locators and 31 chapter locators. They render correctly and should remain, especially where they identify quotations, definitions, or a specific part of a book.

Sources:

- ESE submission guidelines: https://link.springer.com/journal/10664/submission-guidelines#References
- Published ESE example: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10664-022-10204-8
- Bundled Springer Basic style: `template/spbasic.bst`
