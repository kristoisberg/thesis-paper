\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Occurrence-level validation revealed class differences hidden by the pooled score. The selected detector achieved micro F1 \num{0.869}. Per-class F1 ranged from \num{0.481} to \num{0.974}. Across the corpus, ID Required flags were broad and seldom repeated within generated-schema files, whereas Implicit Columns flags were narrower and repeated within application/query files. The 61 highest-count repositories contained \qty{59.1}{\percent} of flags and \qtyrange{48.1}{49.6}{\percent} of relevant files, with \numrange{1.47}{1.56} times the remaining flag density. Exact-content sensitivity preserved both findings. Recurring \texttt{selectFrom}, \texttt{select()}, and \texttt{like(} fragments provide concrete strata for audit.

These findings connect class-specific occurrence agreement to the breadth and repetition of detector output. The next step is an independent corpus audit of flagged and unflagged uses within the identified fragment strata.
