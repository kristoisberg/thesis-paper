\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Occurrence-level validation revealed class differences hidden by the pooled score. The selected detector achieved micro F1 \num{0.869}. Per-class F1 ranged from \num{0.481} to \num{0.974}. Across the corpus, its \num{15931} flags were concentrated by class and repository: Implicit Columns and ID Required accounted for \qty{68.3}{\percent}, and the highest-count \qty{10}{\percent} of flagged repositories contained \qty{59.1}{\percent} of the output. Recurring \texttt{selectFrom}, \texttt{select()}, and \texttt{like(} fragments provide concrete strata for audit.

These findings connect class-specific occurrence agreement with recurring forms in a large jOOQ corpus. The next validation step is an independent corpus audit of flagged and unflagged uses within the identified strata.
